Added a §5 entry ("Reaction-half execution: hand-rolled kernels vs delegating to LockstepODE.jl"). Key facts it records: the state-blocked layout is byte-identical to LockstepODE Batched `PerIndex` (ode = node, ode_size = M), so the seam is free; delegating outsources P2/P3 and buys the AMDGPU/Metal/oneAPI backends. Cons: the OS split wants an RHS function while LockstepODE is integrator-level (nesting one inside a substep reopens A4 and the Rush–Larsen seam), and LockstepODE hard-depends on full OrdinaryDiffEq. Recorded stance: `PointwiseODEFunction` stays as the thin OS-facing adapter; its execution engine delegates to LockstepODE, contingent on the 0 B/call + inference contract holding and on LockstepODE exposing its RHS-level engine without the OrdinaryDiffEq dep. Also split the §7 non-goal so multi-simulation batching is no longer conflated with this role, and added a §9 traceability row.
